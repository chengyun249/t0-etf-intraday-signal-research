# Negative results

- VWAP mean reversion was not robust after realistic friction.
- Long-only pair reversion was weak; long-short variants face shorting constraints.
- ORB and noise-boundary filters produced thin and unstable margins.
- Walk-forward parameter updates did not establish a stable tradable edge in the historical experiments.
- The corrected 2025 commodity subset remains positive at 2bp but has only 28 test trades and turns negative by 5bp.

These findings are part of the deliverable. Statistical classification or IC metrics are not evidence of economic value unless a leakage-aware execution test survives realistic costs.
